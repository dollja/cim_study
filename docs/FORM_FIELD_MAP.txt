CURRENT FORMAT NOTICE — CIM_REFERENCE_KEYS_V1

The material below describes the previous form-label encoding and the unchanged original form captions. It is retained as a crosswalk, not as the full current exported structure. Current REVIEWS.json / REVIEWS_r1_v5_reference_keys.json uses the requested paths documented in docs/REFERENCE_KEY_GUIDE.txt: subtask_hypotheses[].description, focus.quote/utterance_id/why_selected, participant_interpretations, consequence_analysis, rationale and selection_reason. Other readable fields and inner reading/consequence labels are retained. The form imports all three encodings and exports the reference-key format. The new optional why_selected field is JSON-only; no new form field was added.
