\subsection{Preparation and software validation}
The current pilot materials contain 20 proposed episode records: ten MapTask
records from ten dialogues on four map pairs and ten Werewolf transcript
records. These records constitute a candidate review set. Recovery or approval
of an earlier final selection has not been established. At the time of this
package update, no inclusion decisions or adjudicated reference labels had
been completed, and no external model inference was performed.

The implementation was synchronized with the 20-slide integrated early-warning
deck dated 6 November 2026, including its speaker notes. Human review uses
Tier S expressed evidence and preserves the distinction between a pending
label and a substantive \texttt{UNRESOLVED} judgment. Availability of a landmark
and a bare acknowledgment do not independently establish adoption of a referent.
Earlier explicitly grounded speech may nevertheless support the active reading.

The new tests and records are separate from the archived 26-test record.
A constructed supplied-record replay produced \texttt{POTENTIAL\_MISMATCH}.
Six additional code replays on retained dialogue prefixes produced four
\texttt{ALIGNED} and two \texttt{UNRESOLVED} outputs. Their meanings, attribution
records and semantic support checks were supplied rather than inferred by a
model. Three alignments remain conditional on draft map context, and the MT03
cutoff follows a correction. These outputs demonstrate execution of stipulated
records; they do not estimate model accuracy, establish reference labels or
measure warning lead time.

The review exporter binds adjudicated references to the frozen observer input.
The reporter excludes a run from scoring when that canonical input hash differs
or the reference is unbound. Independent, assisted-development and unspecified
legacy reference conditions remain distinguishable. Schema validity and hash
agreement alone do not establish semantic correctness or actual reviewer
independence.
