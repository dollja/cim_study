\subsection{Reproduction of software and input-validation checks}
\label{sec:cim5-fresh-software-validation}

On 2 November 2026 (UTC), the existing pilot workbook was checked in an
isolated working copy using Python 3.12.14. All 26 automated software tests
passed. The preparation validator also passed for all 20 prospective
candidate records: 20 source-blob hashes matched, 20 prefix--suffix
reconstructions were complete, and 20 first-response boundaries passed
the implemented checks. All 20 observer input objects passed the
implemented checks for exclusion of curator fields. These byte and
structure checks do not establish semantic correctness, transcription
accuracy against original media, sample independence, or the adequacy
of reference annotations.

All 20 candidates remained unselected, and all 20 candidate inputs
containing draft context were correctly blocked by the model-run
preflight. The inventory comprises
10 MapTask and 10 Werewolf candidates; this inventory does not establish
that the originally requested 20 selections have been recovered or approved.

The constructed replay, adapted from the slide example with both
anchors present on both maps, returned \emph{Potential-Mismatch}.
Six further replays used real dialogue prefixes but supplied the
discovery, meaning, attribution, and semantic-support records in
advance. Their deterministic F5--F7 computation returned four
\emph{Aligned} and two \emph{Unresolved} decisions. Three of the four
alignment results depended on unverified map inventories. The
constructed fixture and the six real-prefix replays were explicitly
marked as non-model outputs; the real-prefix replays were also marked
ineligible for the primary evaluation. They demonstrate execution on
supplied records and do not test model discovery, interpretation
coverage, or independently assessed detection accuracy.

No language model was invoked, and no human annotation review was
performed during this check. Detection precision, recall, coverage
under actual model execution, and warning timing therefore remain
\emph{not measured}. The command logs, exit codes, source-code hashes,
validation output, and replay records are preserved in the accompanying
verification archive. The exact starting workbook is identified by
its SHA-256 digest in \texttt{execution\_summary.json}.
